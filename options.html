Custom URLs

Regular Expression Guide

Common Patterns
  • ^https?://localhost:\d+/.* - Any localhost with port number
  • ^https?://.*\.example\.com/.* - Any subdomain of example.com
  • ^https?://.*\.zoom\.us/j/\d+.*#success$ - Zoom success pages
  • ^https?://app\.myservice\.com/session/[a-f0-9-]+$ - Dynamic session IDs
Regex Symbols
  • ^ - Start of URL
  • $ - End of URL
  • .* - Match any characters
  • \d+ - Match numbers (like port 3000, 8080)
  • [a-f0-9-]+ - Match specific characters (like UUIDs)
  • \. - Match literal dot (escape with \)
Tip: Use \ to escape special characters. Test your patterns carefully!
